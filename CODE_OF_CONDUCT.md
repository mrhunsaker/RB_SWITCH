# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to build and document an accessibility device used by students, paraprofessionals, teachers, and caregivers. We want the community around it — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space — to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Given the subject matter of this project, we hold an especially high standard around respectful, non-stigmatizing language when discussing disability, assistive technology, and the students and users these tools serve.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and levels of technical background — not everyone contributing to this project is a firmware or hardware engineer, and documentation and classroom-use feedback are as valuable as code.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it the same way: focused on the work, not the person.
- Show empathy toward other community members, especially when discussing the needs of the students and users this device serves.
- When describing disability or assistive technology use, use accurate, respectful, person-first or identity-first language as appropriate to context, and avoid language that stigmatizes disability or frames assistive technology as a burden.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private communication tied to the project.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory or off-topic comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information (physical or email address, school, employer, or similar) without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances, in any project space.
- Mocking or dismissive language about disability, assistive technology users, or the students this project serves.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication channel officially associated with this repository), and also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private security advisory or by contacting the maintainer listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All reports will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's other maintainers.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct was written for this project, drawing on widely used community-standards conventions found in many open-source projects (including the general structure popularized by the Contributor Covenant), adapted for a project whose primary audience includes educators and assistive-technology users.
